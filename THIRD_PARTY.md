# Third-party runtime resources

- DejaVu fonts: font files and their license are under `src/code_visualizer/resources/fonts/`.
- SPARTA native participant: the DLL, license, manifest, data and corresponding source ZIP are under `src/code_visualizer/resources/sparta/`. The source ZIP belongs to that bundled native participant and is retained with its license; it is not the Tcl DRAKON editor.

Saved physical examples and packaged example resources are retained so example projects remain portable.
