# Reading structural DRAKON in Code Visualizer

The straight vertical backbone is the **skewer**. Read it from top to bottom to follow the ordinary path. A connected construct with one entry and one normal continuation is a **block**. The secondary orthogonal route from a decision is a **liana**. A complete typed composite such as an If is a **macroicon**; inserting it creates its required branches and join at once.

An **insertion boundary** is the semantic place between ordered operations. The canvas projects a boundary onto every eligible visible route segment, including horizontal segments. The broad pointer corridor is intentionally larger than the thin line. A **valence** states what can legally attach there; an illegal structural route never becomes executable just because two shapes touch.

## Preferred path and truth

The downward route is the preferred or “happy” path: the expected, successful, or easiest-to-read course stays visually straight. An exceptional route goes right. Down does not mean True.

For `if cache_hit`, True may be preferred and remain down. For `if request_is_invalid`, False may be preferred and remain down while True goes right to reject the request. Each port therefore displays both its Boolean truth and whether it is preferred. Python generation uses those stored truth bindings and emits an inverted test when False is the preferred first suite.

## Metre, rhythm, air, and common fate

At 100% zoom, `U` is 16 device-independent pixels. Neighboring icons retain at least `2U` of clear vertical metre. Sibling routes retain at least `6U` of horizontal rhythm. Text receives `U` of horizontal and `0.5U` of vertical air. Related branch starts align by common fate, while ordinary icons share an adaptive body width and exact skewer coordinate.

These are layout constraints, not decoration. Text measurement happens before outlines, dividers, ports, and routes are solved. Fit-to-content includes connectors and labels as well as icons. Unmodified wheel or two-finger motion pans; Ctrl/Cmd+scroll, native pinch, or explicit controls zoom.

## Shelf

The Shelf is a state-change operation, not generic storage. Its upper compartment identifies the assignment target (and optional annotation/operator). Its lower compartment computes the value. Each compartment is measured independently and neither text region can consume the divider's reserved air.
